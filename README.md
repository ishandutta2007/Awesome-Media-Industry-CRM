# Top Media Industry CRM Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Advertising Sales, Subscriber Lifecycle & Audience Monetization*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Media Industry CRM Tools**. These tools help publishers, broadcasters, and media companies manage advertising sales, subscription lifecycles, content rights, audience engagement, and advertiser relationships — unifying fragmented systems across digital, print, TV, and streaming channels.

**Examples** include Salesforce Media Cloud, WideOrbit, Imagine Communications, OPERATIVE, Matrix Solutions, Mirada Media, Naviga, Lineup Systems, Plannet Media, and Mediaocean (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom media workflows, and transparent audience data — ideal for independent publishers, small newsrooms, and developers building data-sovereign media CRM platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Utopía CRM](https://github.com/utopia-media/utopia-crm)**  
  Open-source CRM built by Uruguayan newspaper La Diaria with Google News Initiative support. Combines CRM, CMS, newsletter management, audience engagement, and payment tools specifically designed for small and mid-size media organizations. Includes subscriber management, promotional tracking, and communication modules [citation:1]. Free for publishers seeking technology independence.

- **[Ghost](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost)**  
  Open-source publishing platform with native memberships, subscriptions, and newsletters. While primarily a CMS, Ghost includes built-in CRM features for managing subscriber relationships, tiered access, and engagement analytics — used by independent journalists, publishers, and media companies [citation:5][citation:11]. MIT licensed, self-hosted or managed.

- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  
  Open-source marketing automation platform with lead management, campaign builder, email marketing, and CRM integrations. Suitable for media organizations managing audience engagement and subscriber nurturing [citation:2][citation:9]. Supports segmentation, personalization, and multi-channel campaigns.

- **[Cream CRM](https://github.com/Campware/Cream)**  
  Open-source CRM specifically designed for media organizations. Tracks sales orders, payments, subscriptions (online and print), promotional campaign effectiveness, and customer communications with template-based newsletters. Built on PostgreSQL with Apache Tomcat [citation:8][citation:18].

- **[X2CRM](https://github.com/X2Engine/X2CRM)**  
  Comprehensive open-source CRM with marketing automation, sales force automation, and customer service tools. Features workflow automation, analytics, email marketing, social media integration, and mobile-friendly design [citation:14][citation:19].

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  
  Open-source CRM with customizable entities, workflow automation, and email integration. Adaptable for media organizations managing advertiser relationships, content contributors, and subscriber data.

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  
  Open-source CRM fork of SugarCRM with modules for sales, marketing, and support. Can be customized for media industry use cases including advertising sales tracking and subscription management.

- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Open-source ERP with CRM, subscription management, and invoicing modules. Adaptable for media companies managing advertiser contracts, subscription billing, and content licensing.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)** — Open-source Laravel CRM with lead management, pipeline tracking, and email integration for media sales teams.
- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)** — Modern open-source CRM with contacts, companies, and opportunities management for media advertiser relationships.
- **[Vtiger CRM](https://github.com/vtiger-crm/vtigercrm)** — Open-source CRM with sales, marketing, and support modules adaptable for media workflows.
- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)** — Open-source ERP/CRM with modules for invoicing, proposals, and customer management.
- **Analytics Tools** — Matomo, Plausible, and Umami for privacy-focused audience analytics.
- **Newsletter Tools** — Listmonk, phpList, and Keila for media subscriber communications.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Ghost** for publishing and memberships, **Utopía CRM** for media-specific subscriber management, **Mautic** for audience engagement automation, and **Matomo + Metabase** for audience analytics to build a complete self-hosted media CRM stack.
